## Problem Statement

<h4 id="take-the-length-l-and-breadth-b-of-the-rectangle-as-input-and-find-its-area">Take the length(L) and breadth(B) of the rectangle as input and find its area.</h4>

<h5 id="note">Note:</h5>

<pre><code>Length and breadth must be an integer value and the area will always be in the range of integers.
</code></pre>

<h5 id="input-format">Input Format:</h5>

<pre><code>The first line of input contains two integers, both separated by a single space denoting the length and breadth respectively.
</code></pre>

<h5 id="output-format">Output Format:</h5>

<pre><code>The only line of output prints the area of the rectangle
</code></pre>

<h5 id="constraints">Constraints:</h5>

<pre><code>1 &lt;= L, B &lt;= 10ˆ2
</code></pre>


<h5 id="sample-input-1">Sample Input 1:</h5>

<pre><code>4 20
</code></pre>

<h5 id="sample-output-1">Sample Output 1:</h5>

<pre><code>80
</code></pre>

<h5 id="explanation-of-sample-input-1">Explanation of Sample Input 1:</h5>

<pre><code>Length of the rectangle is 4 and breadth is 20. 
Hence the area of the rectangle is (length*breadth). 
So the answer is 4*20=80.
</code></pre>
